# trislab.com: build brief

For the development team. Generated by `handoff-plan` from the analysis in tl-product-ideas and copied to the product repository as `docs/brief.md` by `handoff-apply`. The product repository is public: no client contacts, budget, prices, offers or personal names.

## What to build

Trislab's corporate site, rebuilt on `tl-template-website` in the repository `trislab-website-corporate-site`. It presents what Trislab offers (custom development, cloud services, Netwrix security solutions and the industry digitization platform), the Netwrix partnership and the company, and lets visitors contact sales. There is no sign-in, no stored data and no form: contact is by e-mail links.

## Pages or modules

Pages are built from the template's sections (`page-sections`):

- **Home:** heading, Netwrix partner badge, services and products in short, contact sales.
- **Services:** one page per service: custom development, cloud services, Netwrix solutions.
- **Products:** one page per launched product (the industry digitization platform) and per Netwrix pillar (data security, password compliance, PAM and IAM, identity threat detection and response), plus a NIS2 compliance page.
- **Partners:** Netwrix as the official partner. Other technologies are shown without logos unless their use is confirmed.
- **About:** the story, "absolute visibility", key numbers.
- **Contact:** address, e-mail by topic, a link to the map and to Trislab's Google Business profile (`contact-without-backend`).
- **Imprint, privacy policy, accessibility statement** (`legal-pages`).

Content collections (`content-collections`):

- `services`, about 3 items; `products`, about 5 (the platform and the four Netwrix pillars). New items come rarely.
- Fields per item: title, summary, icon, body, call to action; for products also a status (live, pilot, planned) and a link.
- The texts come from the current site's code (`tl-company-webapp`, `lib/i18n.ts`), tightened in English first.

## Languages

English (default), Slovenian, Croatian, Serbian and Italian. Every language has its own URL with `hreflang` and `x-default` (`multi-language`). Translations are an AI draft from English, reviewed by a native speaker before production.

## Domains and environments

- **Main address:** trislab.com. www.trislab.com redirects to it permanently (`redirects`, at the Cloudflare edge). trislab.si is not registered and gets no redirect.
- **The current site** has no sub-pages to keep, so no page redirects.
- **Environments:** `dev` (stamp `corporate-site-dev` in `products-sandbox-neu`, region `northeurope`), which replaces the current `staging` stamp; every merge to `main` deploys to it. No test, staging or demo environment. Production in `products-prod-neu` comes later, once the content is approved, with trislab.com and www.trislab.com as custom hostnames; how a version is promoted to it without a release-tier environment is still open with the platform team.
- **Edge (Cloudflare):** trislab.com is a Trislab zone; its mail and other records stay. WAF rules before the first public production hostname, Cloudflare Free plan features only (`edge-waf`); default cache rules (`caching`).
- **Release:** pull request with a green `ci / ci`, merged to `main`, which deploys to `dev`; production later, when the content is approved by Trislab's release approver (`deployment-pipeline`). The steps are in the template's `docs/deployment.md`.

## Integrations and outside services

None. No analytics for now, no outside fonts, maps or embeds: the Content-Security-Policy stays at the template's default (`'self'` only).

## Quality baseline

All of the template's baseline except uptime alerts, which are not needed: `responsive-layout`, `accessibility` (WCAG 2.1 AA and a statement), `performance-budget`, `page-weight-budget` (almost no JavaScript), `image-optimisation`, `caching`, `compression`, `security-headers`, `error-pages`. Browsers: current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Assets

- **Received:** the logo (`app/icon.svg`, `components/logo.tsx` in `tl-company-webapp`), the brand colours and font (there is no brand guide), the texts in five languages, the share image (`app/opengraph-image.tsx`).
- **Missing:** the Netwrix logo file (permission to use it is confirmed); the legal texts from Trislab's shared legal pack.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every page above exists in all five languages, with `hreflang`, a canonical address and a sitemap entry.
- `ci / ci` is green: the build, the page-weight budget and the `security.txt` expiry check pass.
- The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from another origin.
- `dev` shows the site, and the release approver accepts its content.
- Google's Rich Results Test reads the structured data, and the browser console shows no Content-Security-Policy errors.
- Each page stays within the page-weight budget; the measured figures are reported back for the budget review.
- Before production: the legal texts from the legal pack replace the drafts, the Netwrix logo file is in place, a native speaker has reviewed the four translations, and the WAF rules are live.
