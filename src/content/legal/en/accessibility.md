## Our aim

Trislab wants this website to be usable by everyone. It is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

## Status

The site has not yet been assessed against WCAG 2.1 AA. This statement will be updated with the result of the assessment.

## Feedback

If something on this site is hard to use, write to [info@trislab.com](mailto:info@trislab.com). Tell us the page and the problem, and we will answer.
